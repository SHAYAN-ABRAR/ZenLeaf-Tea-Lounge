About

About this demo

ZenLeaf Tea Lounge is a working example of a small café website: a menu, a cart, guest checkout, table requests and a staff area. The business itself is imaginary.

How this online demo works

  • This edition runs entirely in your browser. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which serves files but can't run a server or a database.
  • Your cart, orders, table requests, messages and newsletter sign-ups are saved in this browser's local storage. They aren't sent anywhere, other visitors can't see them, and they stay until you reset the demo or clear your browser data.
  • The staff area is open to everyone here, so you can try both sides: place an order, update it as staff, and watch the order page change.

The full version

The same site also runs as a Django application with a SQLite database on the server. There, orders from every guest arrive in one place and staff sign in with a password. The source code and setup steps are on GitHub.

What's illustrative

  • The teas, prices and availability are examples. Descriptions cover how each style of tea generally tastes; they make no claims about origin or sourcing.
  • There's no physical lounge, street address or phone line.
  • No payment is taken, and no emails or texts are sent. The newsletter list is stored but never mailed.
  • There are no customer reviews or ratings on this site, because there are no real customers.
