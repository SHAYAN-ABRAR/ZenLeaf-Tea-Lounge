About

About this demo

ZenLeaf Tea Lounge is a working example of a small café website: a real menu database, a cart, guest checkout, table requests and a staff area. The business itself is imaginary.

What works

  • The menu, cart, orders, table requests, contact messages and newsletter sign-ups are stored in a SQLite database on the server.
  • Orders and table requests get a reference number and a private status page that updates when staff change the status.
  • Staff sign in to manage the menu and availability, update orders and table requests, and read messages.

What's illustrative

  • The teas, prices and availability are examples. Descriptions cover how each style of tea generally tastes; they make no claims about origin or sourcing.
  • There's no physical lounge, street address or phone line.
  • No payment is taken, and no emails or texts are sent. The newsletter list is stored but never mailed.
  • There are no customer reviews or ratings on this site, because there are no real customers.

Your data in this demo

Anything you enter stays in the local database of whoever runs this copy of the site. Use made-up details if you're just trying it out.
