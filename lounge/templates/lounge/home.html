How it works

Order ahead, or save a table

{% icon 'bag' %} Order for pickup

  1. Pick your teas. Add drinks and small bites to your cart. Sold-out items are marked.
  2. Check out as a guest. Leave a name and an email address. No account or card details; payment isn't taken online.
  3. Follow your order. The confirmation page shows your order number and its status as staff update it.
Start an order {% icon 'arrow-right' %}

{% icon 'calendar' %} Request a table

  1. Choose a time. Pick a date, a time and the size of your party.
  2. Wait for confirmation. Your request is saved as pending until a member of staff reviews it.
  3. Check its status. Your request page shows whether it's confirmed or declined, with any note from staff.
Request a table {% icon 'arrow-right' %}

Brewing at home

A simple brew guide

Hotter water and longer steeping pull out more bitterness, so delicate teas need cooler water. These are general starting points; adjust them to taste.

Suggested water temperature and steeping time by type of tea
TeaWaterSteep
Green70–80°C1–3 min
White75–85°C3–5 min
Oolong85–95°C2–4 min
Black90–100°C3–5 min
Herbal100°C5–7 min
